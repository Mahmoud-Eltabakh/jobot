# Phase 23 Summary — Production Gates and Private-Beta Readiness

## Outcome

The private-beta readiness gate is defined as an evidence-backed operational decision: invitation-only launch, explicit support ownership, cost and retention controls, and no-go conditions for launch failures.

## Decisions captured

- operational evidence is required before invite-only launch
- support ownership and escalation are defined
- AI spend, retention, and export/delete proof are treated as release requirements
- the release gate is owner-controlled, not assumed or automatic

## Transition to milestone closeout

With all five v8.0 phases documented and the release gate defined, the milestone can be audited and closed as a private-beta readiness package rather than an open SaaS assumption set.
