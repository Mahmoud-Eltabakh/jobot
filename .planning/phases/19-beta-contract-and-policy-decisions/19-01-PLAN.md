Lock the private-beta contract for Jobot before any hosted runtime or billing work begins. The purpose is to convert provisional SaaS assumptions into explicit product, source, privacy, and AI-usage decisions that engineering and operations can build against.

Output: a decision memo for the beta customer, source policy, data-processing boundary, usage quotas, and go/no-go gates.

This phase is the first dependency in v8.0. It establishes the beta contract so later Phase 20-23 security, persistence, runtime, and beta-readiness work can be grounded in explicit product definitions rather than assumptions.
</context>

## Execution Constraints

- Do not approve a public launch, paid tier, or team workspace in this phase.
- Treat all product assumptions as provisional until captured with a decision owner or explicit deferral.
- Keep this phase scoped to the beta contract, not implementation of the hosted platform itself.
- Capture evidence and unresolved decisions separately so they remain visible as risk items.
