# Phase 22 Context — Hosted Personal Workflow and Managed-AI Controls

## Why this phase exists

Once tenant isolation and persistence are in place, the product must deliver a coherent private-beta experience: onboarding, personal workspace setup, job discovery, fit review, and AI-generated material handling.

## Core beta user journeys

- create an account and set up a personal workspace
- upload or attach a CV/profile
- discover jobs using source-aware workflow controls
- review fit explanations and match scoring
- generate and edit application materials
- manage exports and deletion of their own artifacts

## Risk areas

- quotas and cost overrun
- provider outage without clear fallback
- silent data loss in generated materials or exports
- confusing user experience around AI consumption and rate limits

## Success signal

This phase is complete when a user can operate a fully hosted private-beta workflow without hidden AI or operational failures.
