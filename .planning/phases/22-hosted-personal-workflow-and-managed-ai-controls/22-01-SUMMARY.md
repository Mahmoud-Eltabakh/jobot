# Phase 22 Summary — Hosted Personal Workflow and Managed-AI Controls

## Outcome

The hosted beta workflow is defined around personal workspace ownership, source-aware job discovery, AI usage transparency, and direct user control over generated output and deletion.

## Decisions captured

- onboarding is designed for the individual user in a private beta
- workflow actions are user-scoped and ownership-bound
- quota tracking and failure states are visible to users
- export and deletion options are part of the hosted beta contract

## Transition to next phase

Phase 23 can proceed once the user workflow and AI controls are accepted as a coherent beta experience and are supported by the operational model.
